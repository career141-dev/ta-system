# Requirement lists, version 1

13 September 2026 · Status: **APPROVED v1**. Decisions set by Claude on Uzmaan's instruction: consistency matters more than the exact choice. Any change creates v2, and assessments are re-run.

These lists define what a suitable candidate means for each job. Once you approve them, three things use them:

1. **Reviewers**, when they mark Shortlist / Maybe / Reject.
2. **The platform**, which checks every requirement against the CV and marks it **evidenced**, **contradicted** or **unknown**.
3. **The final test**, which measures the platform against reviewers using the same list.

**How the lists work**
- **Essential:** a Shortlist needs clear evidence of every essential point. If the CV **contradicts** one, the answer is Reject. If the CV is **silent** on one, the answer is Maybe (ask the candidate), not Reject.
- **Preferred:** raises someone's rank. Missing it never rejects anyone.
- **Not a suitability test:** salary, notice and willingness to relocate are checked at follow-up, not here.
- **Decide:** places where the JD and the job record disagree, or where I've had to interpret. Please answer these.

## 1. Procurement Manager (MELWA, manufacturing)

**The role in one line:** runs end-to-end procurement for a large manufacturing plant, **leads raw material sourcing including overseas suppliers**, and manages a procurement team.

### Essential
| # | Requirement | What counts as evidence in a CV |
|---|---|---|
| E1 | **Procurement is their main job**, currently or very recently | Recent titles or duties in procurement, purchasing, sourcing or commercial buying. Not only logistics, stores, merchandising or finance. |
| E2 | **Raw material sourcing for manufacturing** | Buying production inputs (resins, chemicals, metals, packaging, fabrics, food ingredients…) for a factory. Buying only office supplies, IT, services or trading goods does not count. |
| E3 | **Overseas suppliers / international procurement** | Imports, foreign vendor negotiation, LCs or Incoterms, overseas supplier management. |
| E4 | **Manufacturing or FMCG environment** | Employer is a manufacturer or FMCG company (apparel, food, packaging, cement, tyres, chemicals all count). |
| E5 | **About 10+ years in procurement**, see D1 | Career history adds up to it. A short gap under 10 is Maybe, not Reject. |
| E6 | **At least 5 years managing procurement**: managing the function or a team, not only as a senior executive | Titles like Procurement Manager, Head of Procurement, Senior Manager, or an Assistant Manager with clear team or category ownership. |
| E7 | **Bachelor's degree** (the JD says mandatory) | Any bachelor's degree. The JD lists supply chain, procurement or business "or related field". |

### Preferred
- Engineering spares / plant (MRO) procurement
- ISMM (or CIPS)
- SAP procurement (MM)
- Structured reporting environment: a large or listed company with formal procurement processes

### Level
Manager / senior manager. **Too junior:** a procurement executive with no management scope. **Too senior:** a group CPO or director running several functions (Maybe, since they'd likely be too expensive).

### Not a suitability test
Salary (LKR 500–600k all-inclusive) and location are checked at follow-up.

### Decisions (v1)
- **D1. Experience:** **10+ years of procurement, including 5 in management** (the JD). 8–9 years is Maybe.
- **D2. Location:** Western Province (Colombo / Katunayake). Not a suitability test.
- **D3. Degree:** required for a Shortlist. Strong experience without a degree is **Maybe**.

## 2. Group Chief Supply Chain Officer (Lanka Hospital, healthcare)

**The role in one line:** the CXO who owns the **whole supply chain** (procurement, planning, inventory, warehousing, logistics, distribution) for a hospital group, and reports to the executive committee.

### Essential
| # | Requirement | What counts as evidence in a CV |
|---|---|---|
| E1 | **Has led an end-to-end supply chain**, or at least most of it | Held responsibility for **3 or more** of: procurement/sourcing, demand and supply planning, inventory, warehousing, logistics/distribution. **Procurement alone, or logistics alone, is not enough.** |
| E2 | **Currently or recently at senior leadership level** | Head of Supply Chain, GM, Director, Group Head, CSCO, or DGM with the whole function reporting to them. A Manager without ownership of the full function is Maybe at best. |
| E3 | **Scale:** large teams, significant spend, complex supplier networks | A multi-site or group company, a large team, big spend, or many suppliers. **Often missing from CVs: when silent, that's Maybe, not Reject.** |
| E4 | **About 10 years of progressive supply chain / procurement / operations experience** | Career history. |
| E5 | **Measurable results** in cost, working capital, inventory or service levels | Stated savings, stock reductions, availability improvements. **Often unstated: when silent, that's Maybe.** |
| E6 | **Bachelor's degree** in a relevant or related field | Any relevant degree. |

### Preferred (strongly)
- **Healthcare, hospital, pharmaceutical, medical devices or diagnostics experience**: the JD says "strongly preferred", not required (see D2).
- Knowledge of healthcare supply chains: expiry control, critical stock availability, cold chain, importation of medical items.
- Master's / MBA, CIPS, APICS/CSCP, CPSM.
- SAP / Oracle ERP, supply chain analytics.
- Experience presenting to boards or executive committees.

### Level
CXO / director. **Too junior:** manager or assistant manager level, or a specialist executive. **About right:** Head of Supply Chain at a large company moving up.

### Not a suitability test
Package (LKR 800k–1M+ plus vehicle), travel.

### Decisions (v1)
- **D1. Level:** a Head of Supply Chain at a large company is a **Shortlist** when they own the end-to-end function (E1) at scale (E3). Otherwise Maybe.
- **D2. Healthcare:** **preferred, not required.** A strong FMCG or manufacturing supply chain head can be a Shortlist, ranked below equal healthcare candidates.
- **D3. Degree:** **Bachelor's required, Master's preferred** (the JD).

## 3. Head of Engineering (PGP, glass packaging manufacturing, Horana)

**The role in one line:** leads a **24/7 plant's engineering department** (general maintenance, electrical, instrumentation, utilities), plus projects, reliability, compliance and ESG.

### Essential
| # | Requirement | What counts as evidence in a CV |
|---|---|---|
| E1 | **Plant engineering in manufacturing**: maintenance, utilities, electrical or instrumentation for a production plant | Roles like Engineering Manager, Maintenance Manager, Plant Engineer, Utilities Manager at a **factory**. **Software engineering, building/facilities-only, construction projects, sales engineering and design-only roles do not count.** |
| E2 | **15+ years of engineering experience in manufacturing** | Career history. 12–14 years is Maybe. |
| E3 | **8+ years in a managerial or leadership role** | Titles like Engineering Manager, Maintenance Manager, Head/HOD, DGM Engineering, or clear department leadership. |
| E4 | **Has led a multi-discipline engineering team** (maintenance + electrical/instrumentation/utilities), ideally in continuous (24/7) operations | Department or plant engineering leadership, shift operations, continuous-process plants (glass, cement, tyres, gloves, beverages, paper, power). |
| E5 | **Engineering degree:** mechanical, electrical or mechatronic | BSc Eng or equivalent (see D1). |

### Preferred
- Continuous-process industry: glass, cement, ceramics, tyres, gloves, beverages, paper, power.
- Engineering projects and CAPEX, equipment reliability (TPM, RCM).
- Dealing with CEB, BOI, statutory and environmental compliance.
- ESG / energy management.
- MBA or MSc.

### Level
Senior manager / head of department. **Too junior:** engineer or assistant engineering manager with no department leadership. **Too senior:** plant GM or director of operations who is no longer technical (Maybe).

### Not a suitability test
Package (LKR 400k basic + LKR 240k allowances + variable), and relocation or commuting to Horana.

### Decisions (v1)
- **D1. Degree:** any recognised engineering degree or Chartered equivalent (IESL / IET) is accepted. Where it was earned doesn't matter.
- **D2. Continuous-process experience:** **preferred.** A strong engineering head from any production plant can be a Shortlist.
- **D3. Hotels, hospitals, facilities:** **Reject**, unless the CV shows earlier production-plant engineering leadership, in which case **Maybe**.
